Public domain
=============

One bundled component is released into the public domain by its author rather
than under a licence:

  Cpdf, the R&OS PDF class, shipped inside dompdf at
  Main Files/includes/plugin_pdf/dompdf/lib/Cpdf.php

Its own header reads:

  @license Public Domain http://creativecommons.org/licenses/publicdomain/

There is no licence text to reproduce for a public domain dedication. The
dedication itself is the grant. The header above is preserved in the shipped
file, and the component is listed in third-party-libraries.html with its
author and upstream address so that the attribution travels with the product
even though none is legally required.
