The FAQ area holds questions and answers for your customers. It exists to reduce the
number of tickets you answer twice.

## Writing entries

Each entry is a question and an answer. Write the question the way a customer would ask
it, not the way you would describe the feature — people search with their own words.

## Where customers see it

- **In [the portal](/using/customer-portal/)**, alongside their records.
- **While raising a ticket.** As they type, matching entries are offered. This is where it
  saves the most work.
- **Embedded on your own website**, with the code from _Settings → FAQ_.

## Linking entries to products

An FAQ entry can be attached to one or more [products](/using/products/), so a customer
asking about a particular product is shown the questions that relate to it rather than the
whole list.

## Keeping it useful

The best source of new entries is your own ticket queue. When you answer the same question
a third time, write it up here and link the ticket to it. An FAQ nobody adds to stops
matching how the product actually works within a year.
