A website — or project, which is the same record under a different name — sits under a
customer and groups quotes, jobs and invoices together.

It earns its place when one customer has several separate streams of work. A design agency
with a client who has three sites wants the invoices for each kept apart; without this
layer they all land in one undifferentiated list under the customer.

## Creating one

Open the customer and add a website. The useful fields are the name, the address, and
anything you need to remember about where it is hosted and when things expire.

## Attaching work to it

When creating a quote, job or invoice for that customer, pick the website. From then on
the website's own screen shows everything belonging to it, with its own totals.

## If you do not need it

Plenty of businesses have one relationship per customer and no use for this layer. The
plugin can be switched off in _Settings → Plugins_, which removes the menu and the field
from the forms. See [The plugin system](/developers/plugins/).
